Try messages through sound.

Try the live demo

Trying the live demo and giving feedback in the Project 2c reflection form are required. Attempt to send and receive a short message. If a technical problem prevents a transmission or comparison, describe what you tried and what happened in your feedback.

Open the demo in Chrome on each device and choose your own completed Project 2c notebook on each. The first load downloads Python, NumPy and SciPy, so each device needs an Internet connection. No solution notebook is bundled with this demo.

For phone microphone access, open the demo over HTTPS and allow microphone access when prompted.

Open the demo, choose your completed project2c.ipynb with your completed convolutional encoder, trellis, Viterbi decoder, Part 3 signal helpers and bandpass filter, and wait for Ready. Then type a short message and press Send to hear it. Start with 100 bits/s and a 2000 Hz carrier.

For two devices, open this demo on both devices, load your completed notebook on each and choose the same transmission rate. On the receiving device, press Receive, allow microphone access and wait for “listening.” Send from the other device, wait for the tone to finish, then press Stop on the receiver. Compare the decoded text with the message you sent. Use a short message, nearby devices and comfortable speaker volume; keep both pages open.

Compare Project 2b and Project 2c

Try the same short message in the Project 2b demo and the Project 2c demo. Load your completed project2b.ipynb in the 2b demo and your completed project2c.ipynb in the 2c demo on both devices.

Keep the devices, distance, noise, transmission rate, carrier frequency, filter bounds and volume as similar as possible. Compare how accurately each demo receives the message and how long transmission takes. Describe your observations in the demo feedback question. If a technical problem prevents the comparison, report what you tried and what happened.

Explore further

You can also try the following variations:

  • Move the devices farther apart and try the same message again.
  • Speak near the receiving microphone and see what changes.
  • Try a friend’s phone as the receiver or sender.
  • Adjust the sender’s carrier frequency, the shared transmission rate or the volume. Change one thing at a time to compare the results.

The carrier changes the sender’s tone. Use the same rate on the receiver and keep the carrier inside its filter bounds. After receiving, toggle your bandpass filter or adjust its bounds to decode the same recording again. The original recording stays in this browser until you start a new recording or reload the notebook. This simple demo can produce wrong text or fail to decode, especially with noise.
