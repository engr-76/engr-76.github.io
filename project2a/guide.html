ENGR 76 / Project 2A

Try messages through sound.

Open the demo, choose your completed project2a.ipynb and wait for Ready. Then type a short message and press Send to hear it. Start with 50 bits/s and a 2000 Hz carrier.

For two devices, open the same HTTPS demo link, load your completed notebook on each device and choose the same transmission rate. On the receiving device, press Receive, allow microphone access and wait for “listening.” Send from the other device, wait for the tone to finish, then press Stop on the receiver. Compare the decoded text with the message you sent. Use a short message, nearby devices and comfortable speaker volume; keep both pages open.

Play around

  • Move the devices farther apart and try the same message again.
  • Speak near the receiving microphone and see what changes.
  • Try a friend’s phone as the receiver or sender.
  • Adjust the sender’s carrier frequency, the shared transmission rate or the volume. Change one thing at a time to compare the results.

The carrier changes the sender’s tone; only the rate needs to match on the receiver. This simple demo can produce wrong text or fail to decode, especially with noise. These experiments are optional and ungraded.
